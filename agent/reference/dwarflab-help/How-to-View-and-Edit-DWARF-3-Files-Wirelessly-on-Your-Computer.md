## 1\. Connect your Windows PC/Mac to DWARF 3's WiFi

To access files on the DWARF 3, you first need to connect your Windows PC or Mac to the DWARF 3's WiFi. The **default** WiFi **password** for DWARF 3 is **DWARF_12345678**

> Note: After powering on, DWARF 3 does not automatically generate a WiFi signal. You need to first tap **Connect** in the DWARFLAB app on your phone.

## 2\. Access DWARF 3 Files on Your Computer

Once your computer is connected to DWARF 3’s WiFi, please follow the steps below to access the files.

### 2.1 Access DWARF 3 Files Using a Windows PC

• **Step 1** : Open **This PC** on your computer.

• **Step 2** : In the address bar, enter: ftp://192.168.88.1 to directly access the files.

### 2.2 Access DWARF 3 Files Using a Mac

• **Step 1** : Open **Finder** and click **Go** in the top-left menu, then select **Connect to Server**.

• **Step 2** : In the address field, enter: ftp://192.168.88.1, click Connect and select **Guest** access, and you can directly access the files.

## 3\. Access DWARF 3 Files via FileZilla (Windows PC only)

• **Step 1** : Download and install FileZilla software.

• **Step 2** : Open FileZilla and click **File > Site Manager** in the top-left corner.

• **Step 3** : Select **New Site** , and enter the following settings on the right.

• **Step 4** : Click **Connect** to access the files.

> Note: If file names appear garbled after connecting to the server, go to **File > Site Manager**, select **Charset** , and choose **Force UTF-8**.
