This article introduces the **lighting effects of the DWARF 3** smart telescope in different modes. Please note, switching the shooting mode on the App will **not** change the lighting effects. The lighting effects will only **change after the shooting starts and ends**.

## 1\. Power on/off

### 1.1 Start up

Status: The green circular light will rotate and breathe in turn

### 1.2 Shut down

Status: The red circular light is gradually extinguished

## 2\. Connect

### 2.1 To be connected

Status: Green light strip rotating

### 2.2 Connected

Status: Green light strip solid/always on

## 3\. Photo, Video, Burst Mode

### 3.1 Shooting

Status: Green circular light solid/always on

### 3.2 Object Tracking

Status: Blue-green light breathing

### 3.2 Successful shooting

Status: Green light always on

### 3.3 Shooting failed

Status: Red light is always on

## 4\. Astro Mode

### 4.1 Calibration

Status: Three red light beads gradually lit up

### 4.2 GoTo

Status: Two red light beads light up correspondingly

### 4.3 Tracking

Status: The red light is gradually rotating

### 4.4 Shooting & Stacking

Status: The red light slowly rotates and completes a full circle

### 4.5 Successful shooting

Status: Red light breathing slowly

### 4.6 Shooting failure

Status: Red light flashing rapidly

## 5\. Time-lapse Mode

### 5.1 Shooting

Status: Various colored lights gradually rotate

### 5.2 Successful shooting

Status: Green light always on

### 5.3 Shooting failure

Status: Red light is always on

## 6\. Auto Track Mode

Shooting status: Green light always on

## 7\. Panorama Mode

### 7.1 Shooting

Status: Blue-green light breathing

### 7.2 Successful shooting

Status: Green light always on

### 7.3 Shooting failure

Status: Red light is always on
