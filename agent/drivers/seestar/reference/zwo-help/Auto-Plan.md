# Seestar Auto Plan: Automated Scheduled Shooting Guide

 Table of Contents

The Auto Plan feature automatically generates ready-to-run astrophotography schedules based on real-time sky conditions. You may launch the plan instantly after generation, or manually tweak targets and imaging durations for greater efficiency and flexibility in scheduled shooting.

## Operation Guide

### 1. Enter Planned Shooting

Tap the **Plan** tab on the homepage to open the scheduled shooting page. Tap **Create a plan**, name your schedule, and you will enter the plan editing workspace.

[image: Seestar App homepage Plan entry & Create a plan button interface]

### 2. Auto-Generate Shooting Tasks

On the plan editing page, you can manually add deep-sky targets, or use the new **Auto Plan** tool.

Tap **Auto Plan**, and the system will generate a fully executable shooting schedule matched to current observing conditions. Once finished, you can run the schedule directly or adjust it manually to fit your needs.

[image: Seestar Auto Plan button and auto-generated timeline preview screenshot]

### 3. Adjust Shooting Targets

Once the auto schedule is ready, you can freely edit all targets within the timeline.

**Delete a Target**

Tap any target, then tap the Delete icon at the bottom-left corner. You may also long-press the target to remove it quickly.

**Adjust Imaging Duration**

Select a target and tap the Edit icon at the bottom-right. Drag the timeline slider to change its imaging runtime.

Important note: Target time windows cannot overlap. Save your changes once editing is complete.

[image: Seestar timeline: delete target icon and edit imaging duration adjustment panel]

### 4. Execute the Shooting Plan

After verifying all targets and exposure durations, tap the top-left back arrow to return to the Local Plans list. Locate your newly created schedule and tap **Execute** to start automated guided astrophotography.

[image: Seestar Local Plans page with highlighted Execute button to launch auto shooting]
