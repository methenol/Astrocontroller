# How to Batch Export and Manage Photos on Seestar

 Table of Contents

The Seestar App supports batch photo export, helping you organize captured materials efficiently. You can choose from JPG, FIT, and JPG+FIT formats for viewing, sharing, or performing post-processing later.

## 1. Batch Download Photos from Seestar Album

Open the Seestar App and connect to your device. Go to "My Album" > "Seestar", then open the target folder with the photos you want to export (using the M31 folder as an example).

 [image: Seestar App enter My Album to access stored astro photo folders] [image: Select Seestar album folder to view captured astronomical photos]

Tap the **select checkboxicon **in the upper-right corner, select the photos you want to export, then tap the **download icon**. Choose your preferred format:

• JPG: For quick viewing and sharing.

• FIT: For post-processing and professional analysis.

• JPG+FIT: Saves both preview images and original data for easy organization and post-processing.

[image: Select download option for batch export on Seestar]

[image: Select multiple photos and tap download button in Seestar app]

[image: Choose JPG, FIT or JPG+FIT format for Seestar batch export]

## 2. View Download Progress and Downloaded Files

Tap the **download icon** in the upper-right corner. On the download page, you can view:

• Downloading: Track ongoing download tasks and real-time progress.

• Completed: Browse finished photos/videos sorted by format for quick management.

[image: Seestar download page showing ongoing downloading tasks and real-time progress]

[image: Seestar completed downloads page sorted by FIT, JPG, and MP4 formats]

Completed downloads include three formats: FIT, JPG, and MP4, with different storage rules:

• JPG and MP4: Automatically saved to your phone’s album after download for direct viewing.

• FIT: Not auto-saved to the phone album, requiring manual export or sharing.

## 3. How to Export Seestar FIT Files

Tap the select checkbox icon in the upper-right corner, choose the FIT files you want to export, then tap the share icon. The Seestar App will automatically compress selected FIT files before exporting.

[image: Seestar FIT file download page with select button for batch export]

[image: Select multiple FIT files in Seestar download list for export]

[image: Tap export button to start batch compression of Seestar FIT files]

[image: System sharing menu for compressed Seestar FIT files]

Note: The export sharing interface may vary slightly depending on your phone brand and operating system. The demonstration images are based on a Xiaomi device.

## Important Tips for Seestar File Management

• Downloaded photos and videos can be viewed inside the Seestar App even without connecting your telescope.

• Uninstalling the App or clearing its cache will delete downloaded FIT files. JPG and MP4 files saved to your phone album will not be affected.
