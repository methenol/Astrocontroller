# How to Use Seestar S30 Pro Milky Way Mode

 Table of Contents

Astrophotography has never been this simple — or this powerful.

The new Milky Way Mode offers three creative shooting options: **Capture, Time-lapse, and Star Trails**. With just one tap, you can capture Milky Way details or record the motion of the night sky.

Whether you're a beginner in night-sky photography or an experienced astronomy enthusiast, Milky Way Mode makes it easy to create professional-quality milky way astrophotography images.

#### Three Simple Steps to Start Your First Milky Way Journey
- Power on the device and connect to Seestar (follow the Wi-Fi connection guide).
- Tap "Milky Way Mode", choose a shooting mode (Capture/Time-lapse/Star Trails), and set parameters such as exposure time.
- Tap the shutter button and wait for your final milky way photo.
